- (technique) started Role Playing
- (idea) Homeowner value: find contractors that fit their budget without shopping blind
- (idea) Homeowner value: AI pre-diagnosis saves money/time on in-person consultation visits
- (idea) Homeowner value: discovers new reliable contractors, becomes long-time repeat customer
- (idea) Homeowner value: gets ahead of the curve with a preliminary problem guess before contractor arrives, knows what to expect
- (idea) Homeowner fear: AI misdiagnoses the issue, eroding trust in the whole platform
- (idea) Homeowner fear: gap between AI guess-estimate and contractor's final quote is too wide, leaves them feeling misled/unsatisfied
- (idea) Contractor value: acts like an affordable virtual admin, streamlining intake without hiring staff
- (idea) Contractor value: no more managing requests/answering calls all day
- (idea) Contractor value: AI pre-estimate lets contractor judge job fit before spending time on in-person assessment
- (idea) Contractor fear: cold/unserious leads waste their time and turn them off the platform
- (idea) Need: reciprocal reliability signal - not just customers finding contractors, but contractors able to find/identify reliable, serious customers
- (idea) Lead quality signal: amount/completeness of detail provided (photos, videos, description depth)
- (idea) Lead quality signal: whether customer's stated budget is realistic for the job type
- (insight) Distinguish two budget-mismatch cases: customer doesn't know market pricing (educate) vs intentionally lowballing (bad-fit signal for contractor)
- (direction) Platform is multi-trade long-term (not just plumbing), but Phase 1 will limit scope to a defined subset of trades - details TBD later
- (direction) Tiered pricing model: lower tiers = contractor self-manages dashboard (scales solo business); higher/premium tiers = dedicated admin staff + premium dashboard features. Tier details out of scope for MVP, to be detailed later
- (idea) Dashboard UX: jobs live in a queue of cards showing key details at a glance; click card to expand full detail, photos, videos
- (idea) Admin fear: information overload on the card/detail view makes decisions feel harder, not easier
- (idea) Admin fear: too many steps/clicks to accept or act on a job request causes friction and headaches
- (technique) started Six Thinking Hats
- (idea) Fact: market knowledge is surface-level only, real market research still needed
- (direction) Tech stack direction: AWS hosting; mobile - Swift (iOS) + Kotlin (Android); backend APIs - TypeScript; AI implementation - Python; website - Angular
- (insight) Biggest unknown: how the AI photo/video analysis system will actually work - must be resolved via spike before building anything else
- (idea) Excitement: unique market position - no one else is combining AI photo/video analysis with a seamless, modernized contractor-customer matching flow
- (idea) Excitement: reducing customer stress around expensive, high-stakes home repair work
- (idea) Excitement: empowering small/solo contractors to scale without hiring dedicated admin staff
- (idea) Unease: fear the product ships glitchy, or that core features/AI don't hold up reliably at production scale
- (idea) Best-case 2yr vision: dominant/profitable in a region, becomes the default first-thought platform for home service needs
- (idea) Best-case 2yr vision: repeat usage, not one-time - customers return to the platform for future service needs
- (idea) Best-case 2yr vision: demonstrably scales small contractor businesses and gives them more visibility/attention
- (idea) Best-case 2yr vision: fills a genuine market void, proving LLM-driven active analysis/suggestions can meaningfully improve customers' lives - industry-revolutionizing
- (idea) Risk: a competitor completely copies the concept once proven, eroding first-mover advantage
- (idea) Risk: can't sustain user engagement - users abandon the full intake flow and just find a contractor's contact info to reach out directly, bypassing the platform
- (idea) Risk: contractors gaming the system (fake reviews, self-referrals, manipulating lead/job data) - need a legitimacy/verification system for contractors
- (idea) Green Hat: counter copycats through brand/marketing moat - creative, unique advertising and positioning rather than relying on tech alone
- (idea) Green Hat: track 'reveal contact info' as a logged event tied to that lead - even if homeowner calls direct, the connection is recorded, visible to contractor, and the job can still be tracked/managed through the platform end-to-end (contractor stays anchored to the platform for admin work regardless of how contact was made)
- (decision) Next step after this session: nail down system architecture and define the MVP feature set
- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (direction) Business model: homeowners are free users - platform monetizes via contractors, not homeowners
- (insight) JTBD (homeowner): hired to relieve the anxiety of not knowing what they're looking for or how to find a trustworthy contractor - the job is 'give me confidence and insight to book with certainty', not just 'get a quote'
- (insight) JTBD (contractor): hired to replace the cost of hiring dedicated intake/admin staff at a fraction of the price - the deeper job is 'let me scale my business efficiently without scaling headcount'
- (insight) Deepest JTBD layer (contractor): alleviate the overwhelm of running a business; AI-driven insights should actively help drive revenue, not just cut admin cost
- (technique) started What If Scenarios
- (idea) Ambitious premium AI feature: system learns from historical job costs and materials used to auto-generate more accurate estimates over time - a self-improving pricing engine fed by the contractor's own job history
- (idea) Hybrid intake flow: offer a 'skip the AI diagnosis' path for returning/knowledgeable users who already know what service they need, alongside the full guided AI-assessment flow for new/unsure users
- (idea) If AI accuracy/trust gap vanished: unlocks full smart automation - AI could auto-select which jobs a contractor should pick up and auto-assign/schedule them, not just present leads for manual review
- (technique) started Time Horizon Ladder
- (decision) Timeline goal: MVP live within 6 months
- (idea) 1-year vision: platform live with real paying contractor customers; concrete financial and technical KPIs in place (user count, daily engagement targets)
- (idea) 10-year vision: expand beyond contractors into a general local-services discovery platform (barbers, stylists, mechanics, etc.), all powered by a matured/fortified AI analysis layer that reliably guides users to the right service
- (idea) 100-year speculation: AI becomes advanced enough to autonomously fulfill the underlying need itself, potentially collapsing the 'match human to human via AI' model entirely
- (decision) Converge move 1 (Affinity Clustering): ideas grouped into 6 themes - Trust & Pricing Transparency, Lead Quality & Contractor Trust, Admin Dashboard & Business Ops, Tech Foundation & AI Spike, Growth/Retention & Moat, Vision & Expansion
- (decision) MoSCoW - Trust & Pricing: MUST = AI misdiagnosis disclaimer to homeowner, hybrid skip-AI-diagnosis path; SHOULD = self-improving historical-cost pricing engine
- (decision) MoSCoW - Lead Quality & Contractor Trust: MUST = detail/budget-realism lead signals, cold-lead filtering, contractor legitimacy verification
- (decision) MoSCoW - Admin Dashboard & Business Ops: MUST = card-based job queue with expand detail, low-click acceptance flow; SHOULD = tiered pricing (self-serve vs dedicated-admin), revenue-driving analytics insights
- (decision) MoSCoW - Tech Foundation & AI Spike: MUST = tech stack decisions (AWS, Swift/Kotlin, TypeScript, Python, Angular), AI vision-analysis research spike
- (decision) MoSCoW - Growth, Retention & Moat: SHOULD = contact-reveal event tracking to keep contractor anchored to platform; COULD = brand/marketing moat strategy against copycats
- (decision) MoSCoW - Vision & Expansion: WON'T-THIS-TIME = multi-trade expansion, 10-year local-services vision, 100-year full-automation speculation - pure future direction, not MVP scope
- (insight) Synthesis: hybrid skip-AI-diagnosis flow is the direct mitigation for the trust-gap tension (protects new/unsure users from a bad AI guess while letting knowledgeable users bypass it). Card-queue/low-click dashboard is the literal delivery mechanism for the JTBD 'relieve overwhelm' layer, not just UX polish. Both threads converge on the data flywheel/outcome-logging as the single feedback loop that tightens estimate accuracy AND improves lead-quality filtering - argues for wiring up job-outcome logging early even though the full pricing engine stays a Should
