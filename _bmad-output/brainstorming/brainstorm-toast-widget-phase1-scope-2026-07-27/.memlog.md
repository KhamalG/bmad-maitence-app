- (note) Context: addendum.md already documents this as 'Superseded Framing: Single-Sided Widget Model' (embeddable widget on individual contractor site, no platform-side matching/verification/tiering) previously set aside in favor of full marketplace model; session revives and locks down Phase 1 under this framing
- (technique) started Morphological Analysis
- (idea) Entry point parameter: a button on the contractor's own website that redirects the customer to our hosted platform to submit the request
- (idea) Entry point parameter: also a button/listing on Google, similar to how restaurants have order-through buttons on their Google listing, funneling into the same platform
- (idea) AI response parameter: homeowner should wait at most ~1 minute for a likely-issue response; fast AND accurate, treated as an independently controllable axis
- (idea) Post-submission parameter: request routes to the contractor's own dashboard/intake, where contractor can accept+schedule the maintenance, or communicate with the client to gather more info first -- all actions happen through the contractor's dashboard
- (idea) Capture-depth parameter: widget collects detailed description, photos/videos, plus a dedicated checklist/to-do-list page to help diagnose the issue, then hands off to AI analysis
- (idea) Branding/templating parameter: platform offers templates where contractors add their logo/branding, list the services they offer and job details, can directly request/claim jobs, and can surface customer feedback/reviews from previous jobs
- (idea) Monetization/onboarding parameter: monthly subscription with multiple contractor tiers, plus a mix of self-serve signup and sales-assisted setup to familiarize contractors with the platform
- (idea) Entry-point alt value: customers can also discover and access a contractor's page directly through our own platform/app or via Google search -- independent of the contractor's own website -- similar to how Uber Eats/DoorDash/Toast let diners find a restaurant's ordering page directly, not only through the restaurant's own site
- (decision by user) Discovery-tier crossing: platform-side discovery section would push higher-tier contractors to the top, but tiering itself is NOT needed for MVP -- tiered structure rolls out later alongside more features, after MVP release
- (decision by user) Speed-window scoping: the 60-second target applies only to the AI analysis step itself, not the full intake -- the checklist/description/photo-video capture happens first, untimed, before the AI clock starts
- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (idea) Contractor JTBD (functional): cutting administrative cost -- replaces a hired person for finding leads/work, makes all admin work more seamless
- (idea) Contractor JTBD (emotional): relief from the feeling that their time is being wasted on dead leads and customers who can't communicate the real scope/impact of the work needed
- (idea) Homeowner JTBD (functional): diagnose the issue and get a realistic sense of project cost before ever calling
- (idea) Homeowner JTBD (emotional): lifts the overwhelm of calling a contractor without foundational knowledge of the problem
- (insight) Root homeowner fear behind the JTBD: being overcharged/taken advantage of because they lack the knowledge to know what's fair -- the diagnosis+cost-estimate job is really in service of avoiding that feeling
- (technique) started Build on What Works
- (idea) What works #1: seamless integration into a contractor's existing website, plus a platform-level directory for discovering other providers -- worth expanding on eventually (echoes the platform-discovery entry point)
- (idea) What works #2: platform familiarity itself builds trust and sets clear expectations for the customer -- recognizing the flow because they've used it elsewhere (Toast/DoorDash-style) lowers friction
- (idea) Amplify trust without borrowed brand recognition: accuracy of the AI analysis is the huge seller/differentiator -- make-or-break for the platform, since we don't have Toast/DoorDash-level existing user familiarity yet
- (idea) Amplify further: UI polish and the contractor's ability to 'sell their business' (branding, service listings, reviews) enriches the platform and makes it more desirable to use
- (technique) started Cross-Pollination
- (idea) ER-inspired: visible urgency tags on every job (like ER triage levels), shown to both contractor and homeowner
- (idea) ER-inspired: an algorithm that dynamically readjusts the contractor's job queue ordering as urgent requests come in, not just a static emergency flag
- (idea) AI analysis itself determines the urgency level of a job, not just the diagnosis content
- (idea) Casino-inspired: overall app vibe/look/feel is itself a comfort-and-engagement lever for homeowners, independent of the analysis content
- (idea) Casino-inspired: after a homeowner receives their AI diagnosis naming the likely job, show examples of that same job type plus reviews from other customers (contractor-permitting) right after the analysis, as social proof before they even talk to the contractor
- (insight by coach) Synthesis: center of gravity is (1) platform integration/entry-point model and (2) the AI analysis engine -- everything else (branding, tiering, dashboard) hangs off those two. User's chosen next step: research how the AI engine actually works before locking Phase 1 scope, using addendum.md's existing Rekognition/Bedrock/SageMaker direction as a starting point to validate against the narrower single-contractor scope, not a blank page
- (insight by coach) Synthesis: speed-boundary + accuracy-protection is one design principle (untimed checklist capture exists so the AI clock never has to trade accuracy for speed), not two separate decisions
- (insight by coach) Synthesis: AI-determined urgency tags and post-diagnosis social proof (reviews/past jobs) were each independently invented by two different techniques -- signal they're structural, not nice-to-haves
- (insight by coach) Synthesis: the entry-point parameter's option values (redirect-to-hosted-page -> platform-side discovery/directory -> tiering-on-discovery) already form the phased roadmap from single-contractor widget to fuller platform
