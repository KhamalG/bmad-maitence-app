01 / Technique

Morphological Analysis Deep

Six independent parameters, each pulled apart into its own option values — a spec sheet for assembling the widget, piece by piece.

01

Entry Point

A button on the contractor's own website that redirects the customer to our hosted platform to submit the request.

  • Also a button/listing on Google — similar to how restaurants have order‑through buttons on their Google listing — funneling into the same platform.
Alt value Customers can also discover and access a contractor's page directly through our own platform/app or via Google search — independent of the contractor's own website — the way Uber Eats/DoorDash/Toast let diners find a restaurant's ordering page directly, not only through the restaurant's own site.
02

AI Response

Homeowner should wait at most ~1 minute for a likely‑issue response — fast and accurate, treated as an independently controllable axis.

03

Post‑Submission

Request routes to the contractor's own dashboard/intake, where the contractor can accept + schedule the maintenance, or communicate with the client to gather more info first — all actions happen through the contractor's dashboard.

04

Capture Depth

Widget collects a detailed description, photos/videos, plus a dedicated checklist/to‑do‑list page to help diagnose the issue — then hands off to AI analysis.

05

Branding / Templating

Platform offers templates where contractors add their logo/branding, list the services they offer and job details, can directly request/claim jobs, and can surface customer feedback/reviews from previous jobs.

06

Monetization / Onboarding

Monthly subscription with multiple contractor tiers, plus a mix of self‑serve signup and sales‑assisted setup to familiarize contractors with the platform.

Decision — Discovery‑Tier Crossing

Platform‑side discovery would push higher‑tier contractors to the top, but tiering itself is not needed for MVP — it rolls out later alongside more features, after MVP release.

Decision — Speed‑Window Scoping

The 60‑second target applies only to the AI analysis step itself, not the full intake — checklist/description/photo‑video capture happens first, untimed, before the AI clock starts.

02 / Technique

Job to Be Done Structured

Two personas, each hired for a functional job and an emotional one underneath it.

Contractorhires the widget to…
Functional

Cut administrative cost — replaces a hired person for finding leads/work, makes all admin work more seamless.

Emotional

Relief from the feeling that their time is being wasted on dead leads and customers who can't communicate the real scope/impact of the work needed.

Homeownerhires the widget to…
Functional

Diagnose the issue and get a realistic sense of project cost before ever calling.

Emotional

Lifts the overwhelm of calling a contractor without foundational knowledge of the problem.

Insight — Root Fear

The root homeowner fear behind the JTBD: being overcharged or taken advantage of because they lack the knowledge to know what's fair. The diagnosis‑plus‑cost‑estimate job is really in service of avoiding that feeling.

03 / Technique

Build on What Works Deep

Four ideas, each turning the volume up on the one before it — starting from what already works, amplifying trust step by step.

1
What works

Seamless integration into a contractor's existing website, plus a platform‑level directory for discovering other providers — worth expanding on eventually (echoes the platform‑discovery entry point).

2
What works

Platform familiarity itself builds trust and sets clear expectations for the customer — recognizing the flow because they've used it elsewhere (Toast/DoorDash‑style) lowers friction.

3
Amplify — trust without borrowed brand recognition

Accuracy of the AI analysis is the huge seller/differentiator — make‑or‑break for the platform, since we don't have Toast/DoorDash‑level existing user familiarity yet.

4
Amplify further

UI polish and the contractor's ability to "sell their business" (branding, service listings, reviews) enriches the platform and makes it more desirable to use.

04 / Technique

Cross‑Pollination Creative

Two borrowed worlds, two different comforts: the ER's urgency discipline, and the casino floor's engineered comfort.

ER‑Inspired

  • TRIAGE Visible urgency tags on every job — like ER triage levels — shown to both contractor and homeowner.
  • LIVE An algorithm that dynamically readjusts the contractor's job queue ordering as urgent requests come in — not just a static emergency flag.
  • AI AI analysis itself determines the urgency level of a job, not just the diagnosis content.

Casino‑Inspired

  • The overall app vibe/look/feel is itself a comfort‑and‑engagement lever for homeowners, independent of the analysis content.
  • After a homeowner receives their AI diagnosis naming the likely job, show examples of that same job type plus reviews from other customers (contractor‑permitting) right after the analysis — social proof before they even talk to the contractor.
Closing Synthesis

Where the session landed

Four techniques, one center of gravity. The coach's closing reads, pulled together at the end of the session.

1
Center of gravity

The center of gravity is (1) the platform integration/entry‑point model and (2) the AI analysis engine — everything else (branding, tiering, dashboard) hangs off those two. User's chosen next step: research how the AI engine actually works before locking Phase 1 scope, using addendum.md's existing Rekognition/Bedrock/SageMaker direction as a starting point to validate against the narrower single‑contractor scope, not a blank page.

2
One principle, not two decisions

Speed‑boundary + accuracy‑protection is one design principle — the untimed checklist capture exists so the AI clock never has to trade accuracy for speed — not two separate decisions.

3
Independently reinvented, twice

AI‑determined urgency tags and post‑diagnosis social proof (reviews/past jobs) were each independently invented by two different techniques — a signal they're structural, not nice‑to‑haves.

4
The roadmap was hiding in plain sight

The entry‑point parameter's option values — redirect‑to‑hosted‑page → platform‑side discovery/directory → tiering‑on‑discovery — already form the phased roadmap from single‑contractor widget to fuller platform.
