

The goal of Phase 1 is to become the best AI-powered lead intake platform for contractors.

Our biggest differentiator is an AI photo and video analyzer that lets homeowners upload images or videos of their issue. The AI can identify potential problems, collect all the necessary information upfront, and eventually provide a price estimate.

Initially, our focus should be on contractors. We position the software as something they can integrate directly into their website to generate more qualified leads while eliminating the back-and-forth of answering phone calls and gathering basic project information. By the time a lead reaches them, they'll already have photos, videos, project details, and enough context to decide whether it's a good fit.

Focusing on contractors first also solves a major marketplace problem. We can spend our time perfecting the lead intake experience instead of trying to balance two sides of a marketplace—finding enough contractors for homeowners while also finding enough homeowners for contractors. Once the contractor product is solid, expanding into a consumer-facing app becomes much easier.

One thing worth considering is delaying the AI price estimator. There could be friction if a homeowner sees an AI-generated price range on a contractor's website and treats it as a guaranteed quote. If the contractor ultimately provides a different estimate after an inspection, that could create frustration and hurt trust.

Instead, it might make sense to launch the price estimator as a beta feature. We can gather feedback from contractors, refine the model, and see whether it actually improves conversions. If contractors find it valuable, it could become a premium feature in a higher pricing tier.

Another advantage of starting with contractors is the data we'll collect. Over time, we'll build a proprietary dataset that includes pricing, job types, service areas, project duration, availability, seasonality, and outcomes. That data will continuously improve our AI, making our estimates and recommendations more accurate and creating a competitive advantage that's difficult for others to replicate.

**HOW IT WORKS**

The customer visits the contractor's website and clicks the **"Get Help"** button.  
They enter their basic information, including their name, phone number, email, and address.  
The customer describes their issue in as much detail as possible and uploads photos and/or videos of the problem.  
Our AI analyzer reviews the information and provides a preliminary assessment of what the issue may be. The customer is informed that this is not a final diagnosis and that the contractor has received all of the submitted information and will follow up with an official diagnosis and pricing.  
The contractor receives a fully qualified lead containing:

* Customer contact information  
* Property address  
* Description of the issue  
* Photos and videos  
* AI-generated preliminary assessment
